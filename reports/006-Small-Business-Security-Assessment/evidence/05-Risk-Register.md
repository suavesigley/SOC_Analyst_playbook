# Overall Risk Assessment

**Overall Risk: HIGH**

The organisation has several foundational security controls in place; however, significant gaps remain in authentication, privileged access, endpoint visibility and backup resilience.

The most important immediate objective should be reducing the organisation's exposure to account compromise and ensuring that security incidents can be detected and recovered from effectively.
