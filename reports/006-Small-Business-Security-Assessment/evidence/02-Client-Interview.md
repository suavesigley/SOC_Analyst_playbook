# Identity & Access

### Q: How many employees do you currently have?

**Response**

15

---

### Q: Does every employee have an individual Microsoft 365 account?

**Response**

Yes.

---

### Q: Is Multi-Factor Authentication enabled for all users?

**Response**

No.

Approximately 9 of the 15 users have completed MFA enrolment.

---

### Q: Are dedicated administrator accounts used?

**Response**

The external IT contractor uses a dedicated administrator account.

The Managing Director and Office Manager perform administrative tasks using their standard user accounts.

---

### Q: Are access reviews performed regularly?

**Response**

No formal quarterly review currently exists.

---

### Q: What happens when an employee leaves?

**Response**

The Office Manager disables their Microsoft 365 account.

A documented offboarding procedure has not been established.

---

# Backup & Recovery

### Q: How often are backups performed?

**Response**

Nightly.

---

### Q: Where are backups stored?

**Response**

Encrypted NAS located within the office.

---

### Q: Has recovery testing been performed?

**Response**

A full restoration has not been completed in approximately 18 months.

---

### Q: Are Microsoft 365 mailboxes independently backed up?

**Response**

No.

Management believed Microsoft provided sufficient backup capabilities.

---

# Security Monitoring

### Q: How are security alerts monitored?

**Response**

Users are instructed to notify the Office Manager if Microsoft Defender displays an alert.

No centralised monitoring platform currently exists.

---

# Security Awareness

### Q: Do employees receive cybersecurity training?

**Response**

A short onboarding briefing is provided.

No annual refresher training is conducted.

No phishing simulations have been performed.

---

# Firewall

### Q: What firewall protects the network?

**Response**

Business-grade ISP firewall.

Remote administration is enabled for the external IT contractor.

Firewall logs are not routinely reviewed.

No documented firewall rule review schedule currently exists.
